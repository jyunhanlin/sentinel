# Historical Event Reaction Patterns

Base rates for how crypto (primarily BTC) reacts to major event types.
These are tendencies, not rules — always adjust for current positioning and context.

## FOMC Meetings

### Dot-Plot Meetings (March, June, September, December)

- **Pre-event**: BTC typically trades in a tight range 24-48h before, with declining
  volume as traders wait. Implied volatility spikes 12-24h ahead.
- **During**: Initial move within 5 minutes of announcement (often a fakeout), then the
  real move develops during the press conference 30 minutes later. The press conference
  tone matters more than the decision itself.
- **Post-event**: Volatility typically resolves within 4-8h. Trend established during
  the press conference usually holds for 2-5 days.
- **Typical BTC range**: 3-8% intraday move on dot-plot meetings.

### Interim FOMC Meetings

- Smaller moves (1-3%) unless there's a surprise (rate change when none expected).
- Market focuses on statement language changes, not the decision (usually priced in).
- "As expected" outcomes often trigger relief rallies regardless of direction.

### Key Pattern: The FOMC Fakeout

The initial 5-minute reaction to the statement frequently reverses during the press
conference. Do NOT trade the initial spike — wait for the press conference reaction
to confirm direction. This pattern occurs ~60% of the time.

## CPI Releases

- **Timing**: 8:30 AM ET, pre-market. Crypto reacts immediately; traditional markets
  confirm or deny the move at 9:30 AM open.
- **Surprise threshold**: > 0.2% deviation from consensus is a "surprise." > 0.3% is a
  "large surprise" that typically moves BTC 3-5%.
- **In-line prints**: Often bullish regardless of the actual number — uncertainty removal
  is itself a catalyst. BTC tends to rally 1-2% on in-line CPI prints.
- **Hot CPI + crowded shorts**: One of the most reliable squeeze setups. If funding is
  negative and L/S < 0.7 going into CPI, even a hot print can trigger a short squeeze
  as the "expected" selloff doesn't materialize and shorts cover.
- **Sell-the-news on cold CPI**: If BTC rallied 5%+ into CPI on rate-cut hopes, a cold
  print may trigger profit-taking despite being "bullish." The move was front-run.

### Whisper Number Effect

The "whisper number" (unofficial consensus from rates desks and trading floors) often
differs from the Bloomberg consensus. When the actual print matches the whisper but misses
consensus, the market may react opposite to what the consensus miss would suggest. This is
why "hot CPI → bearish" is an oversimplification.

## ETF Decisions

### Approval Events

- **Pre-event**: The approval is typically front-run for weeks. BTC rallied ~70% in the
  two months before the spot ETF approval in January 2024.
- **On approval**: Classic "sell the news" — BTC dropped ~20% in the two weeks after
  the January 2024 spot ETF approval.
- **Exception**: If the approval is genuinely unexpected (low probability priced in),
  the initial reaction is bullish and holds.

### Rejection Events

- **Expected rejection**: Minimal impact if the market already priced it in (Grayscale
  ETF rejections in 2022-2023 had diminishing market impact each time).
- **Unexpected rejection**: Sharp selloff, typically 5-10%, with recovery beginning
  within 24-48h as the market prices in "delayed, not denied."

### Key Pattern: ETF Flow Lag

After approval, actual fund flows take 1-2 weeks to materialize. The first week of
trading often sees selling from the front-run, then buying pressure from actual flows
creates a secondary rally. Don't conflate the approval event with the flow event.

## Token Unlocks

- **Team/VC unlocks > 5% supply**: Typically bearish, with selling pressure starting
  1-3 days before the unlock (pre-positioning by market makers and shorts).
- **Staking unlocks**: Mixed — many stakers re-stake rather than sell. Less reliably
  bearish than team/VC unlocks.
- **Small unlocks (< 2% supply)**: Usually noise unless the token is low-liquidity.
- **Key nuance**: The sell pressure from unlocks is often front-run. By the time the
  unlock occurs, the selling is partially done. Post-unlock bounces are common.

## Options Expiry

- **Monthly expiry (last Friday)**: Moderate impact. Max pain level becomes a magnet
  for price in the 24-48h before expiry.
- **Quarterly expiry**: Higher impact, especially when combined with futures settlement.
  Can move BTC 2-5% as large positions roll or expire.
- **> $5B notional**: Significant market event. The gamma exposure from options dealers
  creates amplified moves as they hedge/unhedge.
- **Key pattern**: Volatility typically crushes immediately after expiry. Good time to
  enter positions if the directional thesis is intact — you get cheaper entry with
  reduced implied vol.

## Stablecoin Events

- **Depeg events**: Extremely high impact, unpredictable magnitude. UST depeg (May 2022)
  caused a 30%+ BTC crash over a week. USDC depeg scare (March 2023) caused a 10% BTC
  drop in hours, then full recovery in 3 days.
- **Key pattern**: Stablecoin events cascade — one depeg raises contagion fears for
  others. The initial move is almost always an overreaction, but "catching the knife"
  during a stablecoin crisis is extremely dangerous because the tail risk is catastrophic.
- **Recovery**: If the stablecoin re-pegs within 24-48h, the recovery rally is typically
  sharp and overshoots the pre-event price.

## Exchange-Specific Events

- **Exchange hacks**: Immediate 3-10% selloff across all crypto, not just affected tokens.
  Recovery timeline depends on exchange size — Mt. Gox (weeks), FTX (months), smaller
  exchanges (hours to days).
- **Regulatory action against exchanges**: Varies by jurisdiction. US actions (SEC lawsuits)
  cause 5-15% drops. Non-US actions have diminishing impact as the market has become
  desensitized.
- **Listing announcements**: Bullish for the listed token (10-50%+ on Binance listings),
  minimal market-wide impact.

## Compound Event Windows

When multiple events cluster within the same week:

| Combination | Typical Pattern |
|-------------|----------------|
| FOMC + CPI same week | Extended low-volume period before first event, then two waves of volatility. Trade the second event's aftermath, not between them. |
| Options expiry + macro event | Amplified moves due to dealer hedging. Gamma squeeze risk elevated. |
| Token unlock + exchange listing | Often cancel out for the specific token. Market-wide impact minimal. |
| Multiple macro events (CPI + NFP + PMI) | "Data dump week" — market waits for the highest-impact release, others are noise. |
